AI-Powered Fall Detection for Elderly Care

Every year, 7,621 Canadians over 65 die from falls—the #1 cause of injury hospitalization for seniors. EMOTE4D uses a camera and edge AI to detect falls automatically, without wearables or cloud dependency. The system converts video to skeleton tracking in real-time. No face, no body, no video ever leaves the device. Privacy isn't a feature—it's the architecture.

Core Breakthrough
Solving Domain Shift
The gap between lab accuracy and real-world performance is where most AI systems die. We crossed it.
The Problem

Model trained purely on the NTU RGB+D academic dataset achieved 98% accuracy in controlled conditions. Deployed in a real living room? 58% accuracy. Different camera angles, lighting, furniture occlusion, clothing variance, and natural movement patterns destroyed performance. This is the domain shift problem—and it kills most academic CV projects before they ship.

The Solution

Mixed training combining NTU RGB+D dataset with personal validation samples recorded in actual home environments. 42 physics-based features extracted from MediaPipe skeleton data—velocity vectors, center-of-mass trajectories, joint angle derivatives, torso inclination rates. A 9-state posture machine tracks transitions (standing → stumbling → falling → lying) rather than classifying individual frames. Result: 95.8% recall, 97.5% specificity in real-world conditions.

Under the Hood
How It Works

Skeleton Extraction

MediaPipe Pose extracts 33 body landmarks from each video frame at 14.4 FPS. Raw video is never stored—only skeleton coordinates pass to the next stage. This is the privacy guarantee: the system literally cannot reconstruct what you look like.

Physics Features

42 engineered features derived from skeleton data: velocity vectors, center-of-mass trajectory, joint angle derivatives, torso inclination rate, limb extension ratios, vertical displacement acceleration. These encode the physics of falling, not the appearance of a person.

9-State Posture Machine

Instead of classifying single frames, the system tracks state transitions across time: standing, walking, sitting, bending, stumbling, falling, lying, recovering, unknown. A fall isn't a single frame—it's a trajectory through states. This dramatically reduces false positives.

5-Second Confirmation

After detecting a fall signature, the system waits 5 seconds to confirm the person remains in a lying state. If they get up, no alert fires. If they remain down, a Twilio SMS alert is sent to designated caregivers with timestamp and confidence score.

Edge Deployment

Everything runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB). No cloud. No internet required for core detection. Internet only needed for SMS alerts. balenaCloud handles fleet management for multi-device deployments. Per-unit hardware cost: ~$170.

Mixed Training

RandomForest classifier trained on a blend of NTU RGB+D academic dataset (60,000+ action samples) and personal validation samples recorded in real homes. This mixed approach is what solved the domain shift from 58% to 95.8% real-world recall.
