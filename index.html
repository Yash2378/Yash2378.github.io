Blind peer matrix February to April 2026

Pools of 8 to 10 language models answered the same question and then scored each other's answers with model names withheld: 286 evaluations and 22,252 usable scored judgments. My own audit on 16 August 2026 found that 178 of the 500 code answers end inside an unclosed code block, and that 291 of the judgments counted as usable gave a positive score to an answer that was stored empty.

CODE-031 June 2026

Anecdote, not a result. One coding question asked for a fix to a service that confirmed more orders than it had stock for when requests arrived together. Two valid LLM judgments both gave one answer 10.0. Its fix was run with 3 units in stock and 20 requests at once. More than 3 got through in 25 of 25 trials. With the fixes in the two lower ranked answers, that happened in none.
