Primary Focus

EMOTE4D

AI-Powered Fall Detection for Elderly Care

Every year, 7,621 Canadians over 65 die from falls—the #1 cause of injury hospitalization for seniors. EMOTE4D uses a camera and edge AI to detect falls automatically, without wearables or cloud dependency. The system converts video to skeleton tracking in real-time. No face, no body, no video ever leaves the device. Privacy isn't a feature—it's the architecture.

The Multivac

AI Model Evaluation Publication

Daily blind peer evaluation of frontier AI models where models judge each other's responses. A 10×10 peer matrix eliminates single-judge bias—every model answers, then every model judges all responses. Fresh questions daily that can't be memorized or gamed.

xAI officially acknowledged Grok 4.1 Fast's victory in a Multivac evaluation. Category-specific pools cover programming, reasoning, communication, science, analysis, and small language models.
